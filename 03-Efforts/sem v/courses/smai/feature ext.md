things i plan to implement:


**1. Length & Fluency** (`n_words`, `n_sents`)

* Page, E.B. — Project Essay Grade (PEG) framework establishing document length as a primary proxy for quality. (https://www.researchgate.net/publication/286574681_An_overview_of_three_approaches_to_scoring_written_essays_by_computer)

**2. Vocabulary Richness** (`root_ttr`, `hapax_legomena_ratio`, `word_entropy`)

* Johnson, 1944 / Guiraud, 1954 — Root Type-Token Ratio resolving the length-bias of standard TTR. ([sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1075293520300660](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fsciencedirect.com%2Fscience%2Farticle%2Fabs%2Fpii%2FS1075293520300660); arxiv.org/pdf/2606.00250)
* Bhattacharyya et al. — Hapax Legomena Ratio as a measure of lexical diversity. (arxiv.org/pdf/2601.06041; Textanalyzer.io/blog/hapax-legomena)
* Shannon Entropy — Measuring uniform vocabulary distribution across unique word types. (arxiv.org/pdf/2605.21540)

**3. Compression & Redundancy** (`compression_ratio`, `trigram_repetition_rate`)

* Compression ratio as a proxy for statistical redundancy and complexity. (arxiv.org/pdf/2304.12512)
* N-gram self-repetition rate as a combinatorial redundancy metric. (arxiv.org/pdf/2607.00588; arxiv.org/pdf/2504.14218)

**4. Sentence Structure Variability** (`sent_len_std`)

* "Burstiness" (variance in sentence length) as a recognized stylometric signal distinguishing fluent human text. (proofreaderpro.ai/blog/what-is-burstiness-ai-writing; thehumanizeai.pro)

**5. Punctuation & Mechanics** (`commas_per_sent`, `punctuation_diversity`)

* Semicolon counts and punctuation diversity serving as mechanical proxies in PEG-style systems. (files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED458290.pdf)

**6. Readability Formulas** (`fk_grade`)

* Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level index. *(Note: Document this as your deliberate negative result).* (arxiv.org/pdf/2101.10537)
